ENERGY SYSTEM PROBLEM ANALYSIS
==============================

CURRENT SYSTEM:
- Start: 2 energy
- Each turn: refreshPlayerEnergy() resets to MAX_ENERGY (7) — NOT additive
- Max: 7 energy
- Card costs: 0-6 range (38 zero-cost, 88 one-cost, 75 two-cost...)
- Energy steal: baseValue 1-2, compound over 2-3 rounds
- Energy block: prevents energy gain for duration

THE PROBLEM:
Energy refreshes to 7 at start of each turn. BUT energy_steal effects fire
DURING the resolution phase (via resolveActiveEffects), which happens AFTER
the previous turn's cards resolve but BEFORE the new turn's energy refresh.

Wait — actually looking at advanceRound():
1. resolveActiveEffects(gameId, newRound)  ← energy_steal ticks fire here
2. refreshPlayerEnergy(freshPlayer)         ← resets to 7 here
3. drawCard(freshPlayer)

So energy_steal ticks fire, drain energy, then refreshPlayerEnergy resets to 7.
The steal effects are essentially wasted on the between-turn drain.

BUT WAIT — energy_steal also fires as INSTANT effects during card resolution
(resolveLockedPlays). So the flow is:
1. Player A locks in cards
2. Player B locks in cards  
3. All confirmed → resolveLockedPlays fires
4. Each card's effects apply instantly (including energy_steal)
5. After all cards resolve → advanceRound()
6. resolveActiveEffects (compound ticks fire, including energy_steal ticks)
7. refreshPlayerEnergy (resets to 7)

So the REAL problem is:
- During step 4, energy_steal drains energy MID-RESOLUTION
- If Player A plays 3 cards, and the first card's energy_steal drains Player B,
  Player B's remaining cards in the queue still play (they were already locked in)
- BUT the energy drain affects the NEXT turn's available energy only if it
  somehow persists past the refresh

ACTUALLY the real issue is simpler:
- refreshPlayerEnergy sets energy to MAX_ENERGY (7) every turn
- So energy_steal between turns is meaningless — it gets reset
- The ONLY time energy_steal matters is WITHIN a turn:
  - If you steal energy mid-resolution, the target has less for... nothing,
    because locked cards already committed
  - The steal only matters for the ATTACKER gaining energy (capped at 7)

WAIT — let me re-read. The energy_steal fires as an instant effect during
resolveLockedPlays (step 4). Cards are already locked. So draining someone's
energy mid-resolution doesn't prevent their cards from playing.

Then in resolveActiveEffects (step 6), compound energy_steal ticks fire.
Then refreshPlayerEnergy (step 7) resets to 7.

So... energy_steal compound ticks are COMPLETELY WASTED because they fire
right before the energy reset. The only value is the attacker gaining energy.

BUT — the user says "energy depletion was OP and killed the game for 1 player."
This means something IS draining energy effectively. Let me check if
refreshPlayerEnergy is actually called for all players...

Looking at advanceRound lines 1089-1116:
  for (const p of stillAlive) {
    const freshPlayer = ... 
    if (freshPlayer && freshPlayer.is_alive) {
      // Sloth ENDURANCE passive
      await refreshPlayerEnergy(freshPlayer);
      await drawCard(freshPlayer);
    }
  }

Yes, all alive players get refreshed. So energy should reset to 7 every turn.

POSSIBLE EXPLANATIONS FOR THE USER'S EXPERIENCE:
1. Energy_steal instant effects drain energy during resolution, and if a player
   had multiple cards locked, the later cards still play but the VISUAL shows
   0 energy, making it FEEL like they're locked out
2. Energy_block effects prevent energy_gain, but refreshPlayerEnergy bypasses
   this by directly setting current_energy = MAX_ENERGY (doesn't go through
   applyInstantEffect). So energy_block is also broken/useless.
3. The client-side game engine may have different logic that doesn't properly
   reset energy
4. The UI may show stale energy values

SOLUTION APPROACH:
Since energy resets to 7 every turn, the "energy depletion" problem is likely
a PERCEPTION issue or a client-side bug. But we should still make the system
more robust:

1. MINIMUM ENERGY FLOOR: Ensure energy never drops below 1 (can always play
   a 0-cost or 1-cost card)
2. FIX ENERGY_BLOCK: Make it actually reduce max_energy temporarily instead
   of being bypassed by refreshPlayerEnergy
3. ENERGY_STEAL REWORK: Instead of draining current energy (useless), make it
   reduce the target's energy regen next turn (e.g., target gets 5 instead of 7)
4. Or simpler: energy_steal reduces max_energy for 1 turn

RECOMMENDED FIX:
- Add MIN_ENERGY = 1 constant
- In refreshPlayerEnergy: set to max(MAX_ENERGY - energyDebuff, MIN_ENERGY)
- energy_steal: reduce target's next-turn regen by stolen amount (stored as
  energy_debt on game_players)
- energy_block: reduce max_energy by 2 for duration
- This makes energy management strategic without ever locking someone out
